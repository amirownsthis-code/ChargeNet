Platform Capabilities

Engineered for Optimal EV Charging Coordination

ChargeNet integrates data-driven algorithms, machine learning predictions, and real-time station telemetry to solve congestion, eliminate missed deadlines, and prevent transformer capacity overloads.

01

Live Station & Port Discovery

Maintains continuous visibility over charging stations, individual port statuses (Available, Occupied, Charging, Faulted, Offline), connector types (AC Type 2 / DC Fast CCS), and active kilowatt power utilization.

02

Shortest-Path Station Routing

Represents the charging station network as a weighted graph. Employs Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm to recommend the nearest available charging station based on driver location, road distance, and remaining battery range.

03

Intelligent Smart Scheduling

Allocates charging slots using constrained optimization and priority queues. Considers current battery percentage, target energy, and departure deadlines so vehicles leave on time without exceeding total station power capacity.

04

Transformer Load Capping & DLB

Enforces strict electrical capacity limits at every station node. Dynamically redistributes active current across simultaneous charging dispensers so local substation transformers are never pushed past safe operating ratings.

05

Automated Fault Rescheduling

Real-time anomaly detection identifies station or port failures instantaneously. Affected charging sessions are dynamically rescheduled to alternative operating stations across the network without requiring a system restart.

06

ML Charging Demand Forecasting

Machine learning regression models trained on historical charging sessions forecast near-future energy demand and arrival volume for upcoming hours, feeding proactive capacity adjustments directly into the scheduler.

Platform Scope & Architecture

An Intelligent Software Layer for EV Charging Infrastructure

ChargeNet does not manufacture physical charging equipment or own charging stations. Instead, it serves as the essential digital orchestration layer that bridges electric vehicle drivers, independent station owners, and municipal power grids.

By deploying graph-theoretic Dijkstra shortest-path algorithms, deadline-aware Earliest Deadline First (EDF) queueing, and simulated IoT event streaming, ChargeNet prevents substation transformer trips, reduces driver wait times, and maximizes station revenue without expensive electrical upgrades.

The platform provides clean, role-based workflows: EV drivers discover available chargers and request charging sessions; station operators monitor utilization, manage ports, and update operating schedules; and system administrators maintain system-wide performance and fault resilience.
